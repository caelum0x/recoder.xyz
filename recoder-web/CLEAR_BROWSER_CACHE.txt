╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                           ║
║     🔧 HOW TO FIX NORMAL BROWSER 504 ERROR 🔧             ║
║                                                           ║
╠═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                           ║
║  THE PROBLEM:                                             ║
║  Incognito works but normal browser shows 504 errors     ║
║                                                           ║
║  THE REASON:                                              ║
║  Your normal browser has CACHED the old 504 error        ║
║  responses. You need to clear the cache ONE TIME.        ║
║                                                           ║
╠═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                           ║
║  SOLUTION - CHOOSE ONE METHOD:                            ║
║                                                           ║
║  METHOD 1: Complete Cache Clear (BEST) ⭐                 ║
║  ───────────────────────────────────────                 ║
║  1. Close ALL browser tabs and windows                    ║
║  2. Reopen browser                                        ║
║  3. Press: Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Win/Linux)                  ║
║            Cmd+Shift+Delete (Mac)                         ║
║  4. Select:                                               ║
║     - "Cached images and files" ✓                         ║
║     - Time range: "All time"                              ║
║  5. Click "Clear data"                                    ║
║  6. Go to: http://localhost:5173                          ║
║                                                           ║
║  METHOD 2: DevTools Clear                                 ║
║  ────────────────────────                                ║
║  1. Go to: http://localhost:5173                          ║
║  2. Press F12 (open DevTools)                             ║
║  3. Right-click the refresh button                        ║
║  4. Select "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"                  ║
║                                                           ║
║  METHOD 3: Settings Clear                                 ║
║  ──────────────────────                                  ║
║  Chrome:                                                  ║
║    - Go to: chrome://settings/clearBrowserData            ║
║    - Select "Cached images and files"                     ║
║    - Time: "All time" → Click "Clear data"                ║
║                                                           ║
║  Firefox:                                                 ║
║    - Go to: about:preferences#privacy                     ║
║    - Find "Cookies and Site Data"                         ║
║    - Click "Clear Data"                                   ║
║    - Select "Cached Web Content" → Click "Clear"          ║
║                                                           ║
╠═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                           ║
║  AFTER CLEARING CACHE:                                    ║
║                                                           ║
║  1. Go to: http://localhost:5173                          ║
║  2. Wait 10-15 seconds for first load                     ║
║  3. Check console (F12):                                  ║
║     ✅ NO "504 (Outdated Optimize Dep)" errors            ║
║     ✅ NO "Failed to fetch" errors                        ║
║     ✅ App loads completely                               ║
║                                                           ║
║  Your normal browser will now work perfectly!             ║
║                                                           ║
╠═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                           ║
║  💡 WHY THIS HAPPENS:                                     ║
║                                                           ║
║  Browsers cache resources aggressively for performance.   ║
║  When you had 504 errors before, the browser cached       ║
║  those error responses. Even though the server is now     ║
║  fixed, your browser keeps showing the old cached         ║
║  errors until you force it to clear them.                 ║
║                                                           ║
║  Incognito mode works because it doesn't use any cache,   ║
║  giving you a completely fresh start.                     ║
║                                                           ║
║  After clearing cache ONCE, your normal browser will      ║
║  work fine and you won't need to do this again (unless    ║
║  you make major changes to dependencies).                 ║
║                                                           ║
